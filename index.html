The Challenge

Manual Document Verification Is Broken

Organizations process thousands of high-stakes documents every day. Manual inspection creates massive operational bottlenecks and easily misses sophisticated digital alterations.

✕

The Manual Verification Bottleneck

  • Time-consuming & repetitive: Officers spend up to 25 minutes inspecting font baselines, seals, and dates manually.
  • Prone to human error: Subtle pixel alterations, font substitutions, and mathematical mismatches slip past tired reviewers.
  • Difficult to scale: Increasing file volume requires exponentially larger compliance teams without increasing accuracy.
  • Undetected tampering: Modern PDF editing tools produce visual counterfeits that deceive the naked eye.
✓

The Signa Intelligent First Layer

  • Instant OCR & Data Extraction: Extracts name, ID number, dates, and organizations automatically in seconds.
  • Deep Anomaly & Alteration Highlighting: Visually highlights suspicious regions, font kerning mismatches, and compression boundaries.
  • Calibrated Signa Risk Score (0–100): Ranks files as Low, Moderate, or High Risk to prioritize officer queues.
  • Human-in-the-Loop Architecture: Provides AI-assisted indicators so human decision-makers make faster, higher-confidence calls.

Core Capabilities

Built for Real Verification Workflows

Signa provides modular intelligence modules to automate repetitive checks and surface critical risks.

Document Verification

Identify structural discrepancies, issuer inconsistencies, and layout irregularities across credentials, contracts, and certifications.

  • Validates standard document geometry & templates
  • Spots missing mandatory legal clauses or metadata headers

Information Extraction

Automatically extract names, identification numbers, conferral dates, organization credentials, and expiry limits without manual typing.

  • High-precision OCR with multi-font normalization
  • Direct structured output (JSON, CSV, ERP API ready)

Anomaly Detection

The flagship intelligence layer. Visually highlights suspicious regions, spliced dates, replaced photos, and localized compression anomalies.

  • Interactive glowing bounding boxes on altered coordinates
  • Detects subtle baseline font shifts and kerning alterations

Risk Assessment

Replace binary "fake/real" assumptions with a balanced 0–100 risk score that prioritizes borderline cases for human verification.

  • Categories: Low Risk, Moderate Risk, and High Risk
  • Actionable recommendations for compliance review teams
Under The Hood

Grounding cutting-edge deep tech in practical, verifiable computer science.

Optical Engine

OCR (Optical Character Recognition)

Multi-scale text recognition extracts textual values, character positions, and spatial bounding boxes across typed, printed, and machine-encoded documents.

Inference Engine

About Signa

Our Vision & Team

Signa aims to make document verification faster, more accessible, and more intelligent by combining AI with human decision-making.

What Signa Is NOT — Our Responsible AI Commitment

Signa does not claim to make a final legal determination that a document is genuine or fraudulent. Instead, Signa provides AI-assisted analysis and risk indicators for human review. This foundational principle ensures compliance officers maintain final accountability while gaining superhuman detection speed.
